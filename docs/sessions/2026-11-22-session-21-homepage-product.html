2. Homepage: what changed

Before, the homepage explained how information is organised (destination, route, segment, observation) but never showed the planning tools. Now:

  • Specific headline: "Plan the ride / stretch by stretch." The lead says what you actually get. The main button "Plan a route" opens Explore Along My Route; the second is "Browse routes". The seeded hero was untouched, so only it was changed; staff-edited copy is never overwritten.
  • The main feature, working and enlarged (new planner section):
    • Choose a start and a destination from the published destinations; "Show routes and stops" opens /journey with both chosen.
    • Swap and same-place checks behave as on the full page.
    • Beside it is the plan of a real published route: stretches in riding order, recorded km, distance from the start, time estimates, surface and fuel, with a link to the full plan.
    • With no route published, it lists what every plan shows and says none is published yet. It never shows an invented example.
  • The tools (new tools section): routes, trips, places, rider reports, organised rides and preparation, each with one sentence about what it does today and a link to the real page. The icons are Lucide, chosen by link, so they stay consistent. It is a ruled two-column list, not a card grid.
  • Kept: the original "how the road is organised" and "every observation carries its date" sections, now after the product sections.
  • Staff control: both new sections are editable and orderable in the staff portal, and validated on the server: at most one route, only internal or https links.
