Session 26c: texture evaluation

2026-11-22. UI only: no API, schema, data, dependency or permission change. No commit or push.

Decision

All 22 directions of the owner's texture brief were evaluated. The reasons for each are in docs/design/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md, section "Texture". One texture is used: contour lines. There is no supporting texture: none of the others strengthens an editorial road journal about mountain riding, and several would break existing rules (no gradients or glass; photos must be real and of the place shown).

PlacementPurpose
Home freshness band (night)Gives the dark band material and links "dated reports" to terrain.
Site footer (night)A quiet brand signature on every page.
Explore "No photo of this place yet" plateMakes the empty plate look deliberate without showing another place's photo; the words still say there is no photo.

It deliberately does not appear on photos, the hero, sign-in, maps, forms, tables, cards, buttons, account or trip screens, or the staff portal.

Implementation

  • .texture-contours in frontend/src/assets/styles/main.css: a pseudo-element behind the content that masks one static SVG with the night-line token (about 1.4:1 against night). There are no pointer events, no animation and no filters, and it is hidden in forced-colours mode.
  • frontend/public/images/textures/contours.svg: abstract artwork, 37 KB raw and about 16 KB gzipped. Every fifth line is a heavier index line. It is generated by scripts/contours-svg.mjs (deterministic, no dependency).
  • Class added in FreshnessSection.vue, SiteFooter.vue and ExplorePage.vue.
  • Checked by eye at 1440 px and 390 px wide: text stays readable and the lines never sit on controls.
