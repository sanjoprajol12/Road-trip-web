Work completed

  • Backend. A places model with categories, a coordinate check against Nepal in both the API and the database, a status lifecycle, submitter, creator and reviewer, observed and last-confirmed dates, images through the media library (usage-tracked) and structured sources. A place_corrections table and contributions.place_id. Policies use permissions (places.manage, places.publish). Staff decisions write audit rows. No one can review their own suggestion. Duplicates are detected (same category within 150 m, or the same name within 2 km). Rider routes are rate-limited and need contribution rights.
  • Staff portal. The Places screen lists places with search, category and status filters and pages. The editor includes the Nepal map picker, cover and gallery images, sources and a preview on the website. Decisions are publish, unpublish, ask for changes or reject (both with a reason the rider sees) and archive. Each place has a history, and corrections have their own queue.
  • Public site.
    • Discovery page with search, category filter and pages.
    • Detail page: images and credits, description, map, coordinates, nearby places and destinations (straight-line distances), sources and freshness, experiences, save, suggest a correction, share an experience.
    • Suggest page: map picker, plus the rider's suggestions with status and the moderator's note, editable after "changes requested".
  • Maps and journeys. Place markers appear on destination maps, and selecting a marker opens the place. Explore Along My Route lists published places near the route with a straight-line distance. No road distances or times are shown.
  • Bugs fixed.
    • Every Leaflet map drew over the sticky header when scrolled.
    • Hidden resource fields were sent as empty [].
    • The map picker text said "destination" on the Places screen.

Security implications

  • Every place route sits behind places.manage or places.publish (staff) or confirmed-rider middleware with contribution rights and rate limits (riders). Public reads return published places only.
  • Status and moderator notes go only to staff and the submitter. Reviewer identities are never shown to riders.
  • Coordinates are checked in three places: request validation, the database check, and the map bounds in the UI.
  • Rider text is stored and shown as plain text. Corrections never change a place automatically; every decision is audited.

Remaining defects and limitations

  • No real places exist yet; none were invented. Staff must enter them, with sources and licensed images.
  • Riders cannot attach photos directly to a suggestion; images come from the staff media library, and rider photos go on experiences.
  • Places cannot yet be added to trips (trip stops have no place_id). Routes are listed but not drawn on the destination map (L13 partial).
  • Distances are straight-line, computed in PHP. PostGIS (D3) would index them; the plan is ready.
  • Not run: WebKit, axe, Lighthouse, a screen reader.
