What was built

  • Request: the rider re-enters their password, ticks a confirmation, and chooses for every public contribution: Delete, Keep anonymously, or Keep anonymously, remove photos.
    • Nothing is pre-selected, and each choice is explained.
    • A missing or foreign choice is refused.
    • Limited to 5 attempts an hour.
  • Cooling-off (30 days):
    • Nothing is destroyed, and other devices are signed out.
    • The rider can still sign in, read, export, cancel and offer handovers, but cannot post, confirm, join, host or be invited. The public profile is hidden.
    • Signing in does not cancel. Only the Cancel deletion button does, and it needs the password.
  • Handovers:
    • An event can go to an approved organizer who is not suspended, deactivated or leaving.
    • A group trip can go to any member, viewers included.
    • Nothing moves until the recipient accepts. Eligibility is checked again at acceptance, and another rider's offer answers 404.
    • Without an accepted handover:
      • the event is cancelled, kept without an organizer, and its riders are notified;
      • the group trip is deleted and its members are notified.
  • Final run (mrn:accounts:process-deletions, daily at 02:30):
    • One transaction per request. A failure rolls everything back and is retried on the next run, and a finished request is skipped.
    • Choices are applied. A public contribution without a recorded choice is deleted.
    • Confirmations the rider gave are removed and the last-confirmed date is recomputed; the confirmed reports are untouched.
    • Notifications and password reset tokens are removed, and places held on other riders' events go to the waitlist.
    • The minimum record is kept: dates, counts, and choices by contribution id.
  • Files: photo files are queued inside the transaction and removed after the commit. A failed removal stays queued and is retried. A file still used by a photo row or by held evidence is never removed.
  • Moderation evidence:
    • Kept for open cases on the rider's contributions, and for open reports the rider filed on others' content.
    • Readable only with moderation.evidence.view, and every view is audited.
    • Removed after 30 days, or earlier by staff. A database check means a hold can never be extended.
  • Export: a password-protected ZIP holding the profile, contributions with photos, confirmations, reports filed, trips (other members by display name only), events, places, saved items, emergency contacts, notifications and sessions.
    • It is streamed in the response and never stored, so there is no URL to guess or expire.
    • It excludes audit logs, staff-only notes and other riders' private data.
  • Staff: a "Deletions and evidence" page. Deletion requests show dates and counts only (users.deletions.view); the evidence list shows no content until a recorded view.
  • Database safety nets: a contribution can have no author only once it is marked as anonymised, and an event can have no organizer only when it is cancelled. A raw delete of a rider who still has content now fails instead of silently cascading.
