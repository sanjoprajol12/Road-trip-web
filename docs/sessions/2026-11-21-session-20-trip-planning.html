Gap assessment (before any change)

docs/audits/TRIP_PLANNING_GAP_ASSESSMENT.md checks every acceptance criterion against code, schema, API, frontend and tests.

  • Real gaps found:
    • No way to edit a stop in the UI.
    • No per-day view, and no warning for nights without a place to stay.
    • Planned and spent costs not distinguished, and unknown costs impossible to enter.
    • Checklist items not editable.
    • A withdrawn destination silently disappeared from its stop.
  • Working but untested: notes, and changes made by an editor being seen by other members.
  • Not built (documented): arrival and departure times are not modelled.

Work completed

  • Stops and accommodation:
    • Each stop can be edited in place: name, day, overnight and note.
    • A day-by-day view gives dates from the trip start, recorded distance and riding time per day (missing values counted, never filled in) and the overnight stop for each night.
    • It warns about nights without a stay, days after the end date, a list that runs backwards, and stops without a day.
  • Budget:
    • Planned and spent costs, all in NPR. A planned cost may have no amount yet; the total then says it is incomplete.
    • A spent cost needs an amount, enforced by the API and by a database check.
    • Costs can be edited and show "paid by". Removing one asks first.
  • Checklist: items can be edited, ticked and unticked, with "n of m done" progress and empty-section text.
  • Notes: the field says that every member can read them; "Details saved." confirms a save; clearing the field removes them.
  • Map: recorded tracks of route and stretch stops are drawn as grey lines next to the markers, and the caption tells the two apart. Stops without a track get no line. The itinerary and day lists carry the same information for keyboard and screen-reader users.
  • Integrity: a withdrawn or deleted destination now flags its stop. A demoted editor loses edit rights at once.

Database

  • Migration 2026_11_21_100022_add_trip_expense_status: trip_expenses.status (planned or spent; existing rows became planned) and a nullable amount_npr, with the check trip_expenses_spent_amount.
  • Applied to the dev and E2E databases. Rollback and re-apply were verified on E2E.
  • Rollback refuses while any amount is missing, so it never invents one.

What the tests cover

  • Feature tests:
    • Accommodation stops added, moved to another day, edited and removed by editors.
    • Viewers, outsiders and guests refused; bad values refused.
    • An editor's change read back by the viewer.
    • A demoted editor locked out.
    • Planned and spent costs, with the missing-amount rules enforced by the API and the database.
    • Checklist edit, tick and untick.
    • Notes written, changed, cleared and capped at 10,000 characters.
    • Withdrawn and soft-deleted destinations.
    • Recorded tracks only, never a straight line.
  • Browser test (Chromium, Firefox, mobile), with owner, editor and viewer:
    • Overnight stop at a destination, edit, reorder, and the day-by-day warnings appearing and clearing.
    • Track and marker on the map.
    • Planned cost without an amount, then marked as spent with one.
    • Checklist lifecycle, and notes saved and then cleared.
    • The editor changes notes and removes a cost; the viewer sees the result with no controls.
    • No sideways scrolling on phones.
  • Screenshots inspected: day by day and budget, on Firefox desktop and on mobile.

Not done or not checked

  • No arrival or departure times, bookings, availability, payments, budget targets or splits.
  • Edits are last write wins, with no conflict detection.
  • Full recorded tracks are sent to the trip page with no simplification. That is fine for a few routes but would need work for very long ones.
  • Not run: WebKit (system libraries), axe, Lighthouse, a screen reader.
