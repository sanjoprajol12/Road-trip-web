Work completed

  • Staff map picker. On the destination form, staff can click the map to place a destination, drag the marker to adjust it, or type exact values (the marker follows). The map is limited to Nepal, uses the tile and attribution settings, and has a "Remove the position" option.
  • Public destination map. An interactive map shows the destination, its services and nearby destinations, with a text alternative because the lists carry the same information. The new "Nearby" section lists other destinations within 50 km, services within 20 km (with Save) and routes passing within 15 km. All distances are straight-line and labelled. Destinations without a recorded position say so.
  • API. GET /api/v1/destinations/{slug} returns nearby: published items only, found with a bounding box and App\Support\Geo, no PostGIS.
  • Bug fixed. Opening an existing destination or CMS entry for editing crashed the staff form with DataCloneError. It was found in the Playwright trace and fixed with toRaw in three places; regression steps were added.
  • Measurement. Added scripts/requirements-progress.mjs for every future session.
