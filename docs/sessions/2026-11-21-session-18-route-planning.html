Work completed

  • Plan this ride (route page):
    • Stretches in riding order with distance, distance from the start, a staff time estimate, surface and services, plus "ride it in reverse".
    • Totals use only recorded data. There is no time total unless every stretch has a time.
    • Fuel planning shows the longest run with no recorded station, counted conservatively and per direction, with caveats.
    • A warning appears when stretches and the route total disagree.
    • Phones get cards; wider screens get a table.
  • Riding time per stretch: a staff estimate between 0.1 and 48 h, with database checks, an editor field and readable validation messages.
  • Route filters: name, province, maximum recorded distance, surface and recorded track, kept in the address.
  • Service markers (B8): published services only, at recorded positions. Each marker opens that service's details, built as text. Services with no recorded position are labelled in the list.

Calculation checks

  • B4: distances are staff-recorded per stretch, summed in riding order, rounded to 0.1 km, and never summed past a missing value. The sum is compared with the route total (warning above 5%). Comparing a stretch distance with the length of its recorded track is not implemented and not required by B4.
  • B5: the times are staff estimates, labelled "estimate" everywhere, and never computed or shown as travel times. The cumulative time stops at the first stretch without one.
  • E4: gaps come only from recorded stretch distances and recorded fuel services, counting the stretches that hold stations in full. Edge cases are unit-tested: fuel on every stretch, fuel only at an end, no fuel, missing distances, empty and single-stretch routes, rounding. No fuel availability is invented.
