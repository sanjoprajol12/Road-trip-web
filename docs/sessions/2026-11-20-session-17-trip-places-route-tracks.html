Moderator permission audit

Moderators hold neither place permission. places.manage lets staff create drafts and edit riders' pending suggestions; the edits are audited, but they change what the rider wrote. places.publish grants unrestricted publish, unpublish and archive of every place, plus decisions on suggestions and corrections. Neither is safe to give moderators for review only. Proposed (not applied): a places.review permission that decides rider suggestions and corrections only. Details are in AUTHORIZATION_MODEL.md.

Work completed

  • Places in trips.
    • "Add to a trip" works from the place, journey and saved pages, and lists only trips the rider owns or edits.
    • The same place twice in one trip is refused by both the API and a unique index. Unpublished places are refused.
    • The trip page links each stop to its place, destination or service, and says when one was withdrawn.
    • The trip map shows one marker per stop position, by kind, and each marker opens its page. Removing a stop works.
    • Viewers see no edit controls, and the API refuses them.
  • Real route geometry.
    • Staff import GPX or GeoJSON tracks per segment in the route editor. Files are checked: 2 to 5000 points, all inside Nepal, no external XML entities, at most 2 MB. Imports are audited and can be removed.
    • Destination maps draw recorded tracks under the markers, and a line opens its route.
    • The route page draws only recorded tracks. Previously it drew straight lines between checkpoints, which your rules say must not be presented as roads.
    • Routes without a track are listed with "no recorded track: not drawn".

Security implications

  • Trip contents stay member-only: there is no public endpoint, and roles are enforced in Laravel. Viewers are read-only.
  • Place stops accept only published places. Withdrawn places stop exposing their details to trip members.
  • Track import is limited to content.routes.manage, rejects external XML entities, bounds the file size and point count, checks coordinates, and is audited.

Limitations

  • No real tracks or places exist yet. Staff must import tracks and enter places; nothing was invented.
  • Track lines can be clicked with a pointer only. The same links are in the route lists for keyboard and screen-reader users.
  • Not run: WebKit, axe, Lighthouse, a screen reader.
