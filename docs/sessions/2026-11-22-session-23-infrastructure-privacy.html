2. Upload safety

  • Which uploads keep files: only rider contribution photos and the staff media library. Place, CMS and destination images reuse the library; GPX and GeoJSON tracks are parsed into the database.
  • The risk: storage.driver allows only local, which disappears on container or serverless hosts.
  • The design:
    • One effective switch: the storage.uploads_enabled setting combined with an UPLOADS_ENABLED environment variable that can only turn uploads off.
    • Enforced in Images::reencode plus route middleware.
    • In production, the local disk forces uploads off and logs it critically, so they pause and are never lost.
    • The state is exposed in /health and /site. The frontends hide upload pickers; everything else keeps working.

3. Account deletion, export and Q9

Found: 30 references to riders in the schema (17 cascade, 13 set null), plus notifications and the audit log with no foreign key. Deleting a rider today would:

  • hard-delete all their contributions;
  • delete events they organise with other riders' registrations;
  • delete group trips they own for every member;
  • leave notification rows behind;
  • leave photo files on disk.

The design (ready to build once Q9 is answered):

  • re-authentication by password and username, pre-checks, an optional cooling-off period;
  • one resumable transaction, with file deletes after commit and retried;
  • a deletion record and an audit entry holding counts only;
  • documented retention exceptions;
  • a ZIP export over a signed 24-hour link.

Q9 needs your answer: published reports are A deleted, B kept without the name, C kept without the name and without photos, or D the rider's choice. Plus:

  • Q9a: events with registered riders;
  • Q9b: group trips with members;
  • Q9c: safety-hold days;
  • Q9d: cooling-off days.

Nothing is decided.

4. Staff MFA

  • How: TOTP plus 8 hashed recovery codes on the custom staff guard. The libraries are already installed through Fortify, so there is no new package.
  • Sign-in: a password step, then a challenge (5-minute expiry, rate-limited, no code reuse).
  • Setup: enrolment with password confirmation.
  • Rollout: optional first, then required; an environment override cannot be weakened by the setting.
  • Recovery: an administrator reset (audited, sessions revoked) and a break-glass artisan command.
  • Riders: sign-in unchanged.
  • Size: about 1.5 sessions.

5. Contact retention

  • Today: messages are kept forever; staff delete them one by one (audited). The dev database has 0 messages.
  • Plan:
    • placeholder periods (spam 30 days, closed 12 months, open never automatic);
    • a closed_at timestamp and legal holds;
    • a daily purge with dry-run and purge off by default;
    • a counts-only audit;
    • a rider "delete my messages" path.
