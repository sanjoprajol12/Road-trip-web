Security changes

  • Staff have their own accounts, guard, cookie, session table (60 min idle, 12 h max), password broker (links to the staff portal), login and provisioning (invitation or mrn:staff:create-admin). No staff sign-up.
  • Rider credentials are refused at staff sign-in; rider sessions get 401 on every staff route and staff sessions 401 on every rider route.
  • Existing staff were carried over with the same roles and no password; accounts are never linked by email. Explicit rider linking (rider password required) feeds the self-review rule; same-email rider content is refused too.
  • Staff maintenance bypass replaced by signed 30-minute preview tokens.
  • Staff two-factor sign-in not enabled: owner decision AR3.
