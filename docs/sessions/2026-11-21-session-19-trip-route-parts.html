Design (written before the schema change)

docs/architecture/TRIP_ROUTE_PARTS.md extends the existing trip_stops rather than adding a second planning system. It covers:

  • Two nullable columns: route_segment_id (FK, null on delete) and route_part (route, stretch, checkpoint_start or checkpoint_end), with a check constraint.
  • Checkpoints stay segment endpoints. Each is stored once: the start of its stretch, or the end of the last stretch.
  • Duplicates are blocked by two partial unique indexes, backed by API checks that name the repeats.
  • A trip holds either the whole route or some of its stretches, never both, so distances are not counted twice.
  • If staff delete a stretch, the stop keeps its name and the trip says the stretch is gone. The staff edit never fails.
  • Rollback: down() drops only the new objects. It was tested on the E2E database.

Work completed

  • Route page, "Add to a trip":
    • Choose an existing trip or "A new trip", and ride either direction.
    • Add the whole route, or tick stretches and checkpoints in riding order.
    • Each stretch shows its recorded km, time estimate and surface, and says what is missing (distance, time, track).
    • A live total covers the selection. Items already on the trip are marked and disabled.
    • Guests are asked to sign in.
  • Trip page:
    • Each route, stretch or checkpoint stop shows its recorded figures and what is missing.
    • Route totals use recorded values only and count the parts without values.
    • Stops move up and down; removing a route stop asks first.
    • Checkpoints with a recorded position appear on the trip map (green). Stretches are never drawn there.
  • API:
    • POST trips/{id}/route-parts adds parts and PUT trips/{id}/stops/order reorders.
    • Only published routes are accepted, and only that route's own stretches. Owners and editors only.
    • A request adds everything or nothing.
  • Documented rule changes: a generic stop with only route_id is now a whole-route stop and is deduplicated. Route stops cannot have their links changed by PATCH; the name, day, overnight and note stay editable.

What the tests cover

  • Feature tests:
    • Whole route, with recorded totals and the counts of what is missing.
    • Stretches and checkpoints in riding order, either direction, whatever order they were chosen in.
    • The canonical checkpoint ("end of stretch 1" equals "start of stretch 2").
    • Duplicates refused by the API and by the database. Whole route and its own stretches refused together.
    • Draft and archived routes refused, as are stretches of another route.
    • A route unpublished later loses its details; a stretch deleted by staff leaves the stop with a notice.
    • Guest 401, outsider 403, viewer 403 (add, reorder, edit, remove); editor and owner allowed.
    • Reorder refuses stale or foreign lists.
    • PATCH cannot change route links.
    • Destination stops still work alongside route stops. The existing place and service trip tests still pass.
  • Browser test (Chromium, Firefox, mobile), the full rider workflow:
    • Guest prompt, then stretches and checkpoints added to an existing trip, then items marked as already added.
    • A new trip created from the route page with the whole route in reverse.
    • Totals and missing values on the trip, reorder that survives a reload, and the checkpoint marker on the map.
    • Removal: cancel keeps the stop, confirm removes it.
    • A viewer sees the itinerary without controls and cannot choose the trip.
    • No sideways scrolling on phones.
  • Screenshots inspected: the route picker on mobile, and the trip itinerary on mobile and desktop. Riding order, missing values and disabled end buttons all look right.
