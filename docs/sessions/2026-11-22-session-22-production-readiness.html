Session 22: production readiness audit

2026-11-22 · Claude Code (Opus 5.5) · Nothing committed · No migration · No dependency · No remote service · No data changed

Verdict

Not ready for a public launch. The application itself is in good shape:

  • Authorization and privacy are enforced and tested route by route.
  • There are no production dependency vulnerabilities.
  • No WCAG AA contrast failures were found.
  • Both test suites are green.

What blocks launch is outside the code:

  • There is no decided hosting or production database with backups.
  • Uploads would be lost on the planned serverless platform.
  • Riders cannot delete their account or export their data.
  • There is no email provider and no error monitoring.
  • Almost no content is published.

Project progress

  • Verified completion: 67.8% (177 / 261), unchanged.
  • Implementation coverage: 81.9%, unchanged.
  • This was an audit session. The two fixes repair defects in existing features, and no requirement changed status.

Accessibility

Automated, without new dependencies: the new production-audit.spec.ts in both apps, plus the existing quality.spec.ts.

  • Contrast (WCAG AA 1.4.3): 0 failures on 13 public pages, 4 signed-in rider pages (account, rides, a trip, saved) and 13 staff screens. Colours were measured against the real solid background behind the text. Text over photos and over the see-through header is not judged automatically.
  • Focus visible and not obscured (2.4.7, 2.4.11):
    • Fixed: tabbing could leave a focused link under the fixed header on the homepage (Chromium and Firefox). Now fixed with scroll-padding-top.
    • Fixed: date fields lost their ring when focus reached the built-in calendar button. Now fixed with :focus-within, in both apps.
    • After the fixes: no problems on 5 public and 5 staff screens, in Chromium and Firefox.
  • Tap targets on phones (2.5.8): no failures once the spacing exception is applied (standalone text links are 20 px tall with enough space around them).
  • Structure (existing checks): one h1, a main landmark, alt text, labelled controls, named buttons and links, unique ids on 13 public and 13 staff screens; a skip link; reduced motion; no sideways scrolling on phones.
  • Maps: every map has a text alternative (lists with the same information), checked in the earlier map browser tests.
  • Not verified: a real screen reader (NVDA or VoiceOver), axe rules, WebKit and Safari.

  • Not installed, as instructed. CI (--with-deps) would install them on GitHub.

Security and privacy

  • Authorization:
    • RouteAuthorizationTest walks every API route: guests get 401 on every protected route, and riders get 401 on every staff route.
    • Trip privacy, viewer, editor and owner roles, IDOR and staff exclusion are covered by the trip test suites (all passing).
    • Live probe: /trips and /admin/overview answer 401 without a session.
  • CSRF (live): 419 without the token; with it, the request is processed (422 for wrong credentials).
  • CORS: a foreign origin gets no Access-Control-Allow-Origin.
  • Cookies: session cookies are httponly and samesite=lax; secure comes from the production template.
  • New SecurityPostureTest:
    • Security headers on every API response, with HSTS over HTTPS only.
    • Production 500 errors return only "Server Error", without the cause.
    • Uploaded photos lose EXIF metadata, including GPS position.
  • Uploads: size limit, decoded type, dimension limits, re-encoded to WebP. GPX and GeoJSON tracks: 2 MB and an extension check.
  • Rate limits: API, registration, auth email, staff login, contributions, contact, reports and uploads, tested in AuthTest, RiderWorkflowsTest and SettingsTrustAndSuspensionTest.
  • Gaps: account deletion and export (critical), no production uploads switch (critical), staff MFA (high), the PHP version header (low).

Infrastructure

  • Ready:
    • The production env template (.env.production.example).
    • The container image (Dockerfile.vercel).
    • A health endpoint with a database check.
    • Migrations run under a separate owner role, with rollbacks tested each session.
    • Trusted proxies, synchronous queue for serverless, and logs to stderr.
  • Missing: hosting, production Postgres, backups and restore, durable storage, email provider, error tracking and alerting. CI is defined but has never run.

Development data (inspected read-only)

  • What exists: 4 published destinations (Pokhara, Bandipur, Mustang, Rara), marked as dev seed and without coordinates; 1 guide and 1 contribution, both unpublished; no routes, stretches, services, places, events or trips.
  • Minimum to verify the product by hand: 2 destinations with coordinates, 1 route with 3 stretches (distances, a time estimate, one recorded track), 2 services including fuel, 2 places, 1 event, 1 published guide, 1 published road report.
  • How it should be made: clearly fictional names prefixed "[DEV]" with is_dev_seed = true, or real data entered by staff. Not created: owner approval needed, and no real roads or services may be invented.
