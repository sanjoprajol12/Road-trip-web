# My Ride Nepal: Product Vision

**Tagline:** Plan better. Ride prepared. Learn from real journeys.

## What it is
A motorcycle travel planning, rider knowledge and community platform for Nepal. Motorcycles only.

## The organising principle
Useful travel knowledge is filed under **destinations, routes, road segments, services, events and actual journeys**, not poured into a social feed. A rider should be able to answer: *what was reported about this stretch of road, by whom, and when?*

```
Destination ──< Route ──< Segment ──< Observation (road report, service note, experience excerpt)
```

## Who it serves
Guests browsing public information; registered riders; experienced and verified trip contributors; verified event organizers; moderators; administrators. Browsing is open; saving, planning, contributing and joining events need an account.

## What it refuses to be
- Not a social network: no follower graph, likes-as-currency or algorithmic feed as core features.
- Not a live-conditions service: historical reports never appear as current road, weather or access information.
- Not an emergency service: no rescue, dispatch or live GPS tracking.
- Not a booking or payment platform.
- Not a source of invented content: no fabricated destinations, prices, reviews, riders or photographs.

## Success looks like
A rider planning Kathmandu to Mustang finds dated, attributed segment reports, knows which ones are corroborated or officially sourced, sees what is stale, prepares with a checklist, and after the ride files a short report that helps the next rider.

## Six product areas
1. Explore Nepal 2. Routes 3. Rider Experiences 4. Events & Rides 5. Preparation & Safety 6. My Rides. Details: `PRODUCT_SPECIFICATION.md`.
