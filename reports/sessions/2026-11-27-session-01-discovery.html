Main technical decisions (proposed)

  • Drizzle ORM over Prisma: CHECK constraints, partial indexes and row locks are central to this product.
  • Server-side sessions over JWT: suspensions and revocations must apply on the next request.
  • pg-boss on PostgreSQL for jobs: no Redis or extra infrastructure.
  • OpenAPI generated from the Zod schemas; each frontend generates its own types, so there is no shared package.
  • Same-origin proxy: cookies stay first-party and CORS stays closed.
  • Vertical slices: each stage ships the database, API, admin and public screens together.
